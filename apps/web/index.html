FrontierSight connects Meta AI — Muse Spark reasoning and SAM 3.1 segmentation — to an independent deterministic physics engine. Models propose and explain. Geometry decides. Every number carries its provenance: MODEL_DERIVED, DETERMINISTIC, FIXTURE, or ASSUMPTION.

1 · Observe

SAM 3.1 segments and tracks people, forklifts, robots in video. Muse Spark proposes what to look for — it never invents a detection.

2 · Verify

Closest-approach, time-to-collision, continuity and constraint checks run in a seeded, reproducible engine. Uncalibrated video stays in pixel space — no fake metres.

3 · Counterfact

“What if the forklift had slowed 1s earlier?” A deterministic braking simulation reruns the world, Monte-Carlo intervals included, and an independent verifier checks the result.

Architecture

Video / fixture ─► SAM 3.1 segmentation ─► Scene extraction ─► Muse Spark hypothesis
                                                              │
        Evidence packet ◄─ Human review ◄─ Independent verification ◄─ Deterministic counterfactual simulation
        (hash-bound)          (approval gate)      (engine/geometry)        (engine/counterfactual)

Limitations, stated plainly: synthetic fixtures validate the pipeline on synthetic data only; no safety certification is claimed; metric claims require declared calibration. See docs/ in the repo.
